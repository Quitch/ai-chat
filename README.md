# AI Chat

A mod for Planetary Annihilation which enables the AI to talk.

## Installation

You should download and install this mod via the Planetary Annihilation: TITANS in-game [Community Mods](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1417396826).

## Current Features

- Allies will announce which planet they landed on when there are multiple starting planets
- Allies notify you when colonising a new planet
- Allies notify you when launching an invasion
- Allies can report their situation per planet on demand, type "report" in team chat
- Allies report when situation per planet dramatically changes
- Allies notify you when they're driven off a planet
- Allies call for help when their army on a planet is being destroyed
- Allies tell you when they move their commander to another planet
- Allies warn you when the enemy reaches a planet your team holds
- Allies report when they start teching up
- Allies report when they build orbital for the first time
- Allies report when they build a Catalyst, and when they complete a nuclear launcher, Titan, or unit cannon
- Allies warn you about enemy nukes, unit cannons, Halleys, Catalysts, and Titans they have scouted
- Allies warn you when an enemy fleet threatens a planet your team still holds
- Allies tell you which planet an enemy commander is on when they first spot it, and again if it moves
- Allies warn you when a planet starts moving under its own engines
- Allies warn you when a moving planet is heading for a planet where your team has units
- Allies react when you are defeated or when a human on your team destroys an enemy
- AIs taunt on victory
- An enemy AI reacts when an AI it is allied with is defeated
- Supports the Legion Expansion, Bugs, and Exiles factions
